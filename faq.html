ANSWERS

Does Droop upload my media?

Supported Droop tools process images, video and audio locally in your browser. Your media is not uploaded to Droop for those tools.

How many presets can a Free account save?

Free accounts can save up to five presets. Pro accounts are planned to support more saved presets and repeat workflows.

What does a Droop account store?

Your account stores your email, optional display name and tool settings such as saved presets. Droop does not store media processed locally.

Can I use Droop without an account?

Yes. The core browser tools work without an account. An account is for saved presets and future workflow features.

What is Droop Pro?

Droop Pro is the planned paid workspace for creators who repeat the same export work. It will focus on saved presets, batch workflows and faster repetition.
