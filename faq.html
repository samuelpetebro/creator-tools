Short answers about privacy, accounts and the creator workflow.

ANSWERS

Does Droop upload my media?

Supported Droop tools process images, video and audio locally in your browser. Your media is not uploaded to Droop for those tools.

How many presets can a Free account save?

Free accounts can save up to five presets. Droop Pro raises the limit to 100 presets.

What does a Droop account store?

Your account stores your email, optional display name, plan and saved workspace settings such as presets, Creator Profiles, Brand Kits and Workflow Recipes. Droop does not store media processed locally.

Can I use Droop without an account?

Yes. The current browser tools work without an account. A Free account adds saved presets; Pro adds repeat-workflow features.

What is Droop Pro?

Droop Pro is the USD 5/month workspace for creators who repeat the same export work. The launch plan includes up to 100 presets, five batch workflows, Creator Profiles, Brand Kits, Workflow Recipes, Recent Runs with Run Again, a Custom Release Pack builder and preset backup/restore. Real-money checkout is not live yet.

Will the Free tools stay useful?

Yes. Pro is designed to remove repetition rather than lock away the current basic browser tools. The current tools are intended to remain usable without a paid subscription.

Can I cancel Droop Pro?

Yes. Once live billing launches, Pro will be a monthly subscription you can cancel through the subscription management flow. Your access continues according to the billing period and the published Terms.
