ANSWERS

Does Droop upload my media?

Supported Droop tools process images, video and audio locally in your browser. Your media is not uploaded to Droop for those tools.

How many presets can a Free account save?

Free accounts can save up to five presets. Droop Pro raises the limit to 100 presets.

What does a Droop account store?

Your account stores your email, optional display name, plan and saved tool settings such as presets. Droop does not store media processed locally.

Can I use Droop without an account?

Yes. The current browser tools work without an account. An account is for saved presets and future workspace features.

What is Droop Pro?

Droop Pro is the USD 5/month workspace for creators who repeat the same export work. The launch value pack includes up to 100 presets, batch Image Converter and Metadata Cleaner for up to 20 files, and preset backup/restore. Real-money checkout is not live yet.
