# Droop Pro pricing proposal

Updated: 2026-09-18

This is an internal launch proposal, not public pricing. Do not expose a real-money checkout or advertise this price until the Lemon store is activated and the owner accepts the proposal.

## Current product reality

Free keeps all current browser tools and up to 5 saved presets.

The first ready Pro benefit is deliberately narrow:

- up to 100 saved presets
- same current browser tools
- batch workflows remain planned
- history / repeat-export workflows remain planned

That means Droop should launch below mature creator suites rather than price as if the roadmap were already shipped.

## Current market anchors

Checked 2026-09-18 against official pricing pages:

- Clideo Pro: USD 9/month when billed annually (USD 108/year), with a broader paid editing/tool suite.
  - https://clideo.com/es/pricing
- Kapwing Pro: USD 24 month-to-month or USD 16/month billed annually, with 4K export, cloud projects/storage and a much broader editor.
  - https://www.kapwing.com/pricing
- CloudConvert uses usage-based conversion credits rather than a directly comparable creator-workspace subscription.
  - https://cloudconvert.com/pricing

These are reference points, not a claim that Droop is feature-equivalent.

## Recommended v1 launch test

**USD 5/month, monthly only.**

Why this is a sensible first test:

- the ready paid benefit is useful but still small
- it keeps a large gap below mature editing suites
- it matches the price already used during Lemon test-mode validation
- monthly-only keeps the first real billing rollout simpler
- Droop can add annual billing later after retention is measurable instead of guessing a discount now

Do not add a fake strike-through price, countdown, or invented savings claim.

## When to revisit

Review pricing after either:

- 30 days of live billing, or
- 10 real paying subscribers,

whichever comes later.

At that checkpoint, record:

- visit -> tool-use conversion
- account creation rate
- preset save/load frequency
- Pro-plan intent
- paid conversion
- cancellations / refunds
- actual recurring operating costs

## Entitlement policy already implemented

The current billing logic intentionally keeps Pro for:

- on_trial
- active
- paused
- past_due
- cancelled while the paid/grace period still exists

It removes Pro for:

- unpaid
- expired

This should remain the launch policy unless the owner deliberately changes it before Live Mode.

## Annual plan

Recommendation for v1: **do not launch annual billing yet**.

If monthly retention is healthy, add annual billing later with a real, explicit discount derived from observed retention rather than choosing one before there is paid-user data.
