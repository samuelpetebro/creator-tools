## What not to overreact to

Do not interpret the current 0 indexed count in the sitemap report as proof that zero pages are indexed. URL Inspection already confirms otherwise.

Do not rewrite titles/content based on five total impressions. There is not enough search data yet.

Do not repeatedly request indexing just because a newly published URL says `URL is unknown to Google`. The inspected unknown URLs are technically indexable and the sitemap is healthy.
